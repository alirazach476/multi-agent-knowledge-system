# Support SLA

## Response targets
| Plan | First response | Resolution target |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Starter | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Growth | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Enterprise | 1 hour (24/7 for SEV-1) | 8 hours for SEV-1 |

## Channels
- In-app help center for How-to questions.
- Email `support@example.com` for account and billing.
- Dedicated Slack Connect for Enterprise.

## Out of scope for L1
Legal advice, medical advice, and ungrounded model speculation must be escalated — never answered from the knowledge bot alone.
